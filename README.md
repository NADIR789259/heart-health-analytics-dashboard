## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze cardiovascular risk factors across a patient population of ~9,000 records. The dashboard turns raw clinical and lifestyle data into a decision-ready view for spotting high-risk patients and understanding the drivers behind heart disease.

## Key Features
- **KPI Summary Cards** — Total patients, average heart rate, high-risk patient count, average cholesterol, average blood pressure, and diabetes rate at a glance
- **Risk Distribution** — Donut chart showing the split between at-risk and healthy patients
- **Heart Disease by Age Group** — Age-based risk breakdown
- **Cholesterol vs. Blood Pressure** — Relationship view between two major cardiovascular indicators
- **Heart Rate Trends** — Distribution of resting heart rate across the dataset
- **BMI Distribution** — Patient spread across BMI bins
- **Risk Factors Comparison** — Smoking status vs. heart disease outcome
- **Interactive Filters** — Sex, age group, cholesterol level, BP category, smoking status, heart disease risk

## Key Insight
Roughly 30% of patients in the dataset (2,727 of 9,000) fall into the high-risk category for heart disease — highlighting the value of dashboards like this for early-warning and preventive-care workflows.

## Tech Stack
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (calculated measures for KPIs and risk segmentation)
- Power Query (data cleaning and transformation)

## Dataset
Heart disease risk dataset (~9,000 patient records) covering demographics, vitals (heart rate, blood pressure, cholesterol), lifestyle factors (smoking, BMI), and diagnosed heart disease status.
